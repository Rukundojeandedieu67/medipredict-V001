# Assumptions and replacement plan

All values below are explicit prototype parameters, not measured operational policies. They must be replaced with RBC/BTD, hospital, or routed-logistics data before operational use.

| Parameter | Value | Why chosen | How to replace |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simulation span | 730 days | Enough history for later time-based splits and rolling tests | Set `period_days` in `src/medipredict/sim/config/sim.yaml` |
| Default frequency | Daily | Keeps the first dataset manageable while supporting 24/48-hour horizons | Set `frequency: hourly` or pass `--frequency hourly` |
| Start date | 2024-01-01 UTC | Fixed, reproducible calendar anchor; not an assertion about observation dates | Set `start_date` |
| Seed | 2026 | Fixed default for reproducible demo runs | Pass `--seed` or set `seed` |
| National demand anchor | 84,383 / 365 = 231.1863 whole-blood-equivalent units/day | RBC 2024 annual collections are the available dated national unit count; using collections as demand is an explicit proxy, not a measured demand series | Replace with dated, observed hospital demand and keep component/product units distinct |
| District weights | 100% hospital-admissions share | Admissions are the only district-level demand denominator available in the supplied data | Set `district_weight_admissions_blend`; replace with dated facility-level demand or population |
| Blood-group mix | RBC 2024 collected mix, aggregated over five RCBTs | Directly follows the report and excludes the undated, inconsistent `blood-bank.csv` mix | Load later dated, observed demand by blood group; do not silently substitute collection mix |
| Demand distribution | Negative binomial NB2, dispersion 20 | Allows overdispersed integer counts; no observed daily series exists to estimate dispersion | Estimate dispersion from dated demand counts |
| Weekly variation | Weekdays 1.032, Saturday/Sunday 0.92 | Neutral-average illustrative weekly pattern; not estimated from Rwanda data | Replace with measured day-of-week effects |
| Synthetic surge rate | 0.0005 per district-day; O+ 2-6 and O- 1-2 additional units | Rare PPH/trauma-like stress events; parameters are illustrative, not incidence estimates | Estimate from dated facility events, with clinical review |
| Rainfall/road-trauma uplift | Probability 0.0 and fraction 0.0 (off) | No aligned weather/road-trauma series is supplied | Join measured rainfall/trauma data and fit effect before enabling |
| Opening inventory | Common groups: 6 days of expected cover, rounded up. Rare node/group cells: configured 0-2 units | There is no observed initial stock snapshot; this is a simulator starting condition | Replace from observed initial stock snapshots |
| Rare-group classification and local target | Daily mean demand below 0.5 units per node/group is rare; local target defaults to 2 units and may be set from 0 to 2 | Provides a deliberately small local buffer for low-volume groups; not an operationally validated policy | Estimate from observed district/group demand, stock and service-level policies |
| Rare-group replenishment | Reorder toward the 0-2-unit local target from the mapped nearest RCBT; at zero target, an unmet rare-group demand can trigger an order of at least one unit. Road/drone choice and travel times are simulated | Daily stock representation, no real order feed, and requirement for on-demand replenishment; pending shipments count toward inventory position | Replace route mapping, demand trigger, mode selection and lead times from timestamped real shipment data |
| Retired global minimum-stock knob and stockout artifacts | Earlier prototype used `minimum_stock_units_per_node_group` as a tuned global floor that prevented stockouts for rare groups (A-, B-, AB-), which was unrealistic. That floor has been removed. The earlier A+/B+ concentration of stockouts was a simulator artifact, not an operational finding; re-evaluate group patterns under the current policy | Retained here for provenance of prior calibration and to avoid treating its results as real; current recalibration reports each group separately | Do not restore the global floor; validate the replacement policy against observed group-level inventory and unmet demand |
| Calibration status | Best-scoring tested setting: rare local target 2 units and 6-hour processing delay. On 20 seeds, unmet demand is 0.107%; A-, B-, and AB- expiry is 15.438%, 24.017%, and 71.566%; minimum stockout episodes/group is 17. No tested setting meets all three recalibration targets | Results are from the bounded, simulated 0/1/2-unit and 6/12/24/48-hour search; this setting is the documented best-scoring candidate, not a target-passing or operational policy | Expand/revise calibration only with an explicit rationale, and validate against observed inventory and shipment data |
| Frozen inference bundle | `models/v0.1.0/`, trained on the current 20-seed simulated dataset; the manifest records Git HEAD `4471de1dc7e14607c8801c4791b34be49cb46f5e` and SHA-256 digests for all payload files (the manifest cannot contain its own hash). Worktree was dirty when frozen | Preserve the accepted Phase 2 classifier/calibrator pair and prevent unreviewed replacement; the bundle is not validated for operational scoring | Create a new model version after review; do not modify or overwrite a published version |
| Inference defaults | Missing expected lead time and expiring-units-within-7-days are imputed to their training medians; missing recent-surge indicator defaults to 0; missing supply defaults to 0 and marks confidence low. Demand may be estimated from consecutive closing stock, supply and expiries when absent | The canonical upload feed may not include every training feature. Defaulted/estimated inputs are flagged in `quality_flags`; scores remain simulated-model outputs | Replace defaults with validated feed mappings before real operational use; inspect each quality flag |
| Inference watch-list | Default `top_k=5`; output is ordered by 24-hour risk when requested, otherwise the first requested horizon. Records without sufficient history have null model risks and are ranked using days of cover, after model-scored records | Provides a small ranked review queue; it is not a stockout alarm and triggers no transfer | Configure queue size only after human review; no dispatch is automatic |
| Upload input layer | Maximum upload size 25 MiB and maximum 500,000 tabular rows; PDF/DOCX extraction is preview-only and requires human review. Patient-identifying columns are dropped based on their headers | Bounds prototype resource use and avoids automatic ingestion of document tables or patient-level fields | Reassess limits for the deployment environment; review privacy-header rules with the data controller |
| Reorder trigger / target | 4 days / 8 days of expected cover | Selected grid-search candidate; not a documented national policy | Estimate from facility replenishment orders and service-level policy |
| Order quantity noise | ±25% | Explicit variability included in the selected calibration candidate | Estimate from order/receipt records |
| Shelf life | 35 days for all simulated groups | Conservative lower end of the stated 35-42 day red-cell shelf life | Use product-specific expiry dates and inventory batches |
| RCBT mapping | Province to Kigali/Butare/Karongi/Ruhengeri/Rwamagana; hubs represented by Nyarugenge/Huye/Karongi/Musanze/Rwamagana districts | RCBTs are mapped by region; hub districts are geographic proxies for distance lookup | Replace with actual RCBT coordinates |
| Dispatch/processing delay | 6 hours, added before estimated travel | Best-scoring tested setting; daily discretization rounds total lead time up to whole days, and 6-/12-hour settings produced identical results in the 20-seed sensitivity run. Calibration targets remain unmet | Replace with timestamped order/dispatch logs |
| Road travel | Supplied straight-line x1.4 / 40 km/h estimate | Existing reference values are placeholders only | Replace with route-engine or observed transport times |
| Delivery split | 56% of orders sampled as drone; remainder road | Approximate share stated in the project brief | Replace with shipment-level delivery-mode data; current value is sampled per order |
| Drone travel factor | 0.45 of estimated road travel plus the same 6-hour processing delay | Illustrative relative-speed assumption; not measured | Fit using timestamped Zipline/road delivery logs |
| Opening stock accounting | Included in first-period `supply_received`; mode `opening` | Keeps simulated stock inputs auditable and expiry-rate denominator complete | Distinguish opening balances from later receipts when real feeds arrive |
| Extra Kigali nodes | Disabled by default; optional flag adds four Kigali facilities classified as `Referral Hospital` in the Ministry 2023-2024 booklet | Official node names are available, but facility-specific stock and demand are not | Pass `--include-kigali-referral-hospitals`; replace the equal district-admissions split with measured facility-level demand when available |
| Kigali referral demand split | When enabled, divide each affected district's admissions weight equally among its district blood bank and listed referral nodes | No facility-level admission totals are supplied | Load facility-level admission totals and allocate weights directly |
| Scope of reported expiry rate | Expired units divided by all received units including simulated opening stock | Makes the waste percentage interpretable against the inventory supply stream | Keep this definition fixed for comparisons or document any change |

The simulator intentionally does **not** use `fact_blood_usage.csv` to set group shares or the Taiwanese UCI `transfusion.data`.
